Attendance,
without the noise.

Read your DCRUSTM register directly from Samarth — subject by subject, session by session — and know exactly where you stand against the 75% rule.

  1. 01 Direct from Samarth Parallel reads of every enrolled subject. No screenshots, no manual counting.
  2. 02 The 75% rule, made plain Safe bunks and recovery classes calculated from your actual session log.
  3. 03 Local by default Credentials stay in this browser. No attendance record is stored on a server.
